## 1. European regulation & supervision

**ICT is still the weakest SREP score as the 2026 SREP letters land** (Nov). KPMG's November SSM briefing says the ECB has delivered the 2026 SREP results and has published or discussed expectations on ICT risk, AI governance and cyber resilience. ICT governance, cybersecurity and third-party management stay the lowest-scoring area. ([KPMG SSM Insights, Nov 2026](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/xx/pdf/2026/11/ssm-quarterly-newsletter-edition-Nov-2026.pdf.coredownload.pdf))

*Why it matters for us:* Read our own SREP letter's ICT findings next to the 31 Oct AI-cyber action plan. They should map to the same roadmap epics, so the JST sees one plan rather than two.

**Update: PSD3/PSR are still not in the Official Journal** (as of 11 Nov). We found no report of formal adoption or publication since issue 1's item. Once published, the PSR applies about 21 months later, and some payee-verification provisions after 27 months. ([Matheson](https://www.matheson.com/insights/psd3-and-psr-published-whats-changing-for-eu-payment-services/))

*Why it matters for us:* Keep the December–January publication assumption in the payments roadmap. The application date will be fixed by that publication date, so record it in the SAB tracker as soon as it lands.

No material EBA, ESMA, AMLA or DORA publications in the window. The only EBA item was a 10 Nov public hearing on ITS for joint prudential decisions.

## 2. Payments

**Digital euro timeline restated: pilot in H2 2027, issuance possibly in 2029** (5–9 Nov). The ECB's working plan is a 12-month pilot with 36 payment service providers from the second half of 2027. A first issuance in 2029 depends on the regulation being adopted in 2026 and a separate Governing Council decision. Trilogues are under way, with co-legislators aiming to finish by end-2026. ([Payment Expert](https://paymentexpert.com/2026/11/05/ecb-digital-euro-banks-merchants/))

*Why it matters for us:* This follows issue 1's network-provider item. If we want a role in the pilot or early onboarding, the integration design (holdings limits, waterfall and reverse waterfall to IBM i accounts, a TIBCO/APIM façade) needs a 2027 budget line now.

No material Wero/EPI, TARGET2/T2, TIPS or ISO 20022 releases in the window.

## 3. Greece

**Eight Greek banks plan 600+ hires in 2027, led by IT and AI** (9 Nov). Hellenic Bank Association members, including NBG, Eurobank, Piraeus and Alpha, will recruit for IT and AI transformation, digital banking, data analytics, ESG and risk. A joint careers event takes place on 28 Nov at Piraeus 260 in Tavros. ([Greek City Times](https://greekcitytimes.com/2026/11/09/greek-banking-sector-enters-new-era-of-recruitment/))

*Why it matters for us:* Every systemic bank will be competing for the same architects and data engineers in the same quarter. Settle our 2027 headcount and grading for solution and data architects before this event resets salary expectations.

No Bank of Greece or IRIS publications of note in the window.

## 4. Payment & banking security

**Crédit Mutuel Arkéa: fraudulent transfers through a supplier's platform** (confirmed 4 Nov, updates to 8 Nov; Context). Attackers reached the group through an external service provider's technology platform and pushed through transfers that press reports put at about €118m. The bank disputes that figure, says part of the funds were blocked, and reports no customer losses. Nearly all services are back. The provider and the technique have not been disclosed, and some funds reportedly went out through crypto exchanges. ([BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-pull-off-133-million-french-bank-heist-via-service-provider))

*Why it matters for us:* This is a payment-initiation compromise through a third party, the exact scenario DORA's register and TLPT are meant to surface. We should check which vendors hold standing connectivity into our payment paths (TIBCO flows, the SWIFT bureau, card processing). We should also check whether outbound-volume anomaly detection would stop a burst of this size before settlement.

**Patch check: Brotli "Node2Shell" flaw and recent KEV additions** (KEV federal deadline 10 Nov). CISA added a critical Brotli decoder flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue on 27 Oct, with a two-week deadline that has now passed. Recent additions also cover Cisco ASA/FTD, Citrix NetScaler and a Windows WinSock driver flaw. Reports give the Brotli bug several names and an unconfirmed CVE ID, so verify against CISA. ([BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-warns-of-hackers-now-exploiting-critical-node2shell-flaw))

*Why it matters for us:* Brotli sits inside Node, NGINX and many API gateways. Ask the APIM and AKS ingress owners to confirm library versions. NetScaler and ASA are classic edge devices for a bank.

## 5. Solution architecture & backend

**KubeCon NA (9–12 Nov) adds an AI inference and agentic track** (9 Nov). CNCF's flagship has a new track for production AI inference and agent workloads, alongside platform engineering and security. Early vendor launches include an "agent skills" registry and eBPF-based database observability. ([CNCF](https://www.cncf.io/announcements/2026/08/10/cncf-reveals-kubecon-cloudnativecon-north-america-2026-schedule-adds-new-ai-inference-agentic-track/))

*Why it matters for us:* Watch the recordings for model serving on Kubernetes and agent sandboxing before we fix the AKS pattern for internal LLM workloads. Ignite (17–20 Nov) will bring the matching AKS announcements.

**Confluent, now an IBM company, launches "Streamhouse"** (4 Nov; Context). Confluent announced capabilities that join Kafka streams and table formats into one architecture, to keep business context fresh for AI agents and analytics. It builds on Tableflow, which already integrates with Delta Lake and Unity Catalog. ([Confluent](https://www.confluent.io/press-release/confluent-introduces-its-streamhouse-architecture-to-power-ai-with-real-time-business-context/))

*Why it matters for us:* With IBM i and Confluent now under one vendor, we should check concentration risk and the DORA register entry. Streamhouse also overlaps with our Kafka-to-Databricks ingestion, so ask the vendor which layer it expects to own the tables.

## 6. Enterprise AI in banking

**BMO puts an agent-assist tool into its credit-card contact centre** (6 Nov; Context). The tool prepares cases for agents handling Canadian card enquiries and targets 20–30% shorter resolution times. BMO calls the contact centre a testbed for components it will reuse in later agentic applications. ([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/?p=506969))

*Why it matters for us:* It is a useful shape for a first production agent: a human in the loop, a narrow case-prep scope, and reusable building blocks. That compares well with NBG's voice-agent goal of handling more than 50% of contact-centre traffic.

No new EBA, ECB, BIS or FSB AI-governance publications in the window.

## 7. Industry moves

**Crédit Agricole proposes merging CA Italia into Banco BPM** (9 Nov). The "friendly" proposal offers a 20% premium and a €1bn special dividend, and would lift Crédit Agricole's stake to about 45%. Banco BPM's CEO would lead the combined group, Italy's third-largest. Completion is targeted for H2 2027, subject to shareholders and the ECB. ([Morningstar / Dow Jones](https://morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/202611091129/credit-agricole-proposes-merger-of-italian-business-with-banco-bpm-update))

*Why it matters for us:* Cross-group integrations like this become reference cases for core consolidation and for the ECB's view of IT integration risk in M&A. Track how they plan the migration.

**European fintech funding stayed light** (week to 9 Nov). BlackFin counted ten European fintech deals worth €108.4m disclosed, plus small capital-markets and insurtech acquisitions. ([BlackFin Tech Weekly](https://blackfintech.substack.com/p/blackfin-tech-weekly-november-9th))

*Why it matters for us:* There is no vendor-landscape change to act on.

## 8. From InfoQ

### Architecture & Design

**Cloudflare builds a CA for Merkle Tree Certificates** (early Nov). Cloudflare is building a public certificate authority for Merkle Tree Certificates. They aim to cut the handshake bandwidth that large post-quantum signatures would otherwise add to TLS. ([InfoQ AI Architecture news](https://www.infoq.com/ai-architecture/news/))

*Why it matters for us:* The ECB has promised a post-quantum communication. Certificate size is a real constraint for mobile banking and APIM edges, so start a crypto inventory now.

### AI architecture

**Uber's MCP Gateway governs 800+ servers and 5,000+ agent tools** (2 Nov; Context). Uber routes agent-to-tool access through a central MCP gateway that handles registration, authorisation and auditing. ([InfoQ AI Architecture news](https://www.infoq.com/ai-architecture/news/))

*Why it matters for us:* This is the pattern we should standardise before agents multiply: one governed MCP entry point behind APIM, with agent identities rather than borrowed user tokens.
